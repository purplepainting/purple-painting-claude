    Method notes

    How this was measured

    • Rooms traced from wall linework: Garage 101 910 SF (plan says 918), Shop 102 450 SF (plan says 457). Within 1%.
    • Walls integrated face by face along the real gable profile: side walls at 9'-10" eave, gable ends and the partition rise to 14'-10" at each ridge. Area above 12' broken out.
    • The three bays step north to south, so the vaulted ceiling has two vertical gyp faces where the roofs meet (about 57 SF). Easy to miss on a flat plan.
    • CMU vs gyp walls found from the wall-type hatch on A-2.1 and the wall schedule (e4 = 12" CMU, e1 = Hardie with 5/8" gyp inside). West shop wall split 40/60 by eye.
    • Openings deducted from gyp walls: 3 garage doors 10'×8', 2 doors 3'×8', 2 windows 2'-6"×3'-0".
    • Fascia: eaves plus exposed rakes, with rakes hidden where a neighboring bay overlaps. Estimate ±10%.
    • Not measured: exterior slab, exterior CMU above stone veneer, fence. Flagged, not counted.
